# Unity ShaderLab Commands - Cull

## Valid Values

`Cull Back`

- Cull the polygons that face away from the camera
- **Default value**

`Cull Front`

- Cull the polygons that face towards the camera
- Use to turn the object inside out

`Cull Off`

- Draw both sides of the polygons
- Do this for transparent objects
- Such as let the back texture of semi-transparent wall show

## What's Culling

- process of determining what not to draw
