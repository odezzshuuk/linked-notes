# Unity - Lighting

## What It Is

## Glossary

Direct Lighting

- Light that emitted, hits a surface once

Indirect Lighting

- Light that reflected into a sensor, includeing light that hits surfaces several times

Real-time Lighting

- calculating at runtime

Baked Lighting

- Save the lighting result as lighting data
- Performance improvement factors
