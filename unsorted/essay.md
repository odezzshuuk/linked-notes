### Reasoning

Basis of Reasoning

- The human body is very precise
  - Hormones
    - Personality can be influenced by hormones, such as testosterone, which can make people more prone to anger.
    - Sunlight promotes the secretion of melatonin, which affects sleep quality and mood.
  - Pregnancy process
    - Human gestation lasts nine months, during which the fetus develops from a fertilized egg into an embryo. The mother's living environment gradually changes over these nine months.
    - Different tissues form at different stages, and each tissue is responsible for different functions.
- The gender of some animals is influenced by temperature, and humans are also living beings on Earth.

Statistical Evidence

- Astrology suggests that Geminis are intelligent, and Nobel Prize winners are predominantly Geminis.
- People living near the Arctic Circle may become depressed due to the polar night.

Scientific Research

- 2009 study by the University of Connecticut found that people born in May and June are more likely to be extroverted, while those born in November and December tend to be more introverted.

### Conclusion

- There is a correlation between a person's personality, intelligence, and their birth date.
- The lack of scientific evidence is because technology is not yet advanced enough to prove it.
