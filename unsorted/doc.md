# Design Documentation

## Introduction

- This Game is about truck delivery packages to different locations. 

## Game Type

- Resource Management
- Management
- roguelike or loot?

## Gameplay

- The game not focus on driving simulation, but more on the management of the delivery business. 
- This game is about 
  - Managing their fleet of trucks, 
  - Optimizing delivery routes to maximize profits.
  - Fighting against enemies on the road, such as bandits or rival delivery companies.
  - There are barriers on the road that need truck to break through
